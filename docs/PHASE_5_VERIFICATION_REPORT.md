## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 5 transitions the cricket simulation platform from a numerical match feed to an interactive, broadcast-quality 2D match visualizer rendered in **PixiJS v8**. 

The simulation engine maintains absolute authority: every event, ball trajectory, fielder placement, and dismissal originates strictly from deterministic match states generated by `@cricket/simulation-engine`. The PixiJS visualizer serves as a high-fidelity observational layer operating at 60 FPS, with authentic physics interpolation, particle dynamics, camera framing, and a TV broadcast lower-third HUD.

All Phase 5 requirements have been implemented, integrated into Next.js 15 App Router, verified through automated unit and integration suites, and proven clean in production compilation.

## 3. Detailed Component Implementation

### 3.1 Field Geometry (`field-geometry.ts`)
- **Pitch Dimensions:** Scaled accurately to 20.12m pitch length and 3.05m pitch width.
- **Turf Condition Shading:**
  - `GREEN`: Deep emerald turf `#5a7d36` with minimal wear `#4a6b2c`.
  - `TURNING`: Dry scuffed surface `#b5894b` with worn footmarks `#9e733b`.
  - `DUSTY`: Ochre dusty pitch `#c4af81` with loose topsoil `#a89367`.
  - `HARD`: Compact reddish-clay `#c89862` with firm bounce lines `#ab7e4c`.
  - `FLAT`: Light straw-brown batting paradise `#d9b382`.
- **Field Markings:** Accurate 30-yard (27.43m) fielding restriction circle with dotted regulatory perimeter, outer boundary foam cushion with stadium sponsorship banners.

### 3.2 Player Management & Tactical Mirroring (`player-manager.ts`)
- **Tactical Field Presets:** Dynamically positions 9 fielders plus keeper and bowler for:
  - `ATTACKING_CLOSE_CATCHERS`: Slips, gully, short leg, backward point, mid-off/on.
  - `DEFENSIVE_RING`: Evenly spread inner ring fielders saving single runs.
  - `DEATH_BOUNDARY_DEFENSE`: 5 boundary riders (deep midwicket, long-on, long-off, deep square, deep point).
  - `POWERPLAY_RING`: 2 boundary sweepers, 7 ring catchers conforming to ICC powerplay laws.
- **Left-Hand Batter Mirroring:**
  - Automatic off-side/leg-side coordinate inversion across the vertical pitch axis ($X' = -X$) when a left-handed batter (`LHB`) faces delivery. Verified in `match.spec.ts`.

### 3.3 Ball Physics & Trajectory Animator (`ball-animator.ts`)
- **Three-Phase Animation Sequence:**
  1. *Run-up & Delivery Stride:* Bowler approaches bowling crease, accelerates ball to release point at $Y = -10.06\text{m}$.
  2. *Flight & Pitch Bounce:* Parabolic trajectory with apex height determined by delivery length (`YORKER`, `FULL`, `GOOD_LENGTH`, `SHORT`) and bounce restitution coefficient $\epsilon \in [0.45, 0.65]$.
  3. *Exit Stroke & Boundary Travel:* 2D shot trajectory computed from shot angle ($[0^\circ, 360^\circ]$), elevation angle ($[0^\circ, 85^\circ]$), and distance to landing or boundary intercept.
- **Height Scaling & Shadows:** Ball radius scales dynamically with elevation ($r = r_0 \cdot (1 + 0.08 h)$), while ground shadow shrinks and fades proportionally ($1 - 0.05 h$).

### 3.4 Visual Effects & Particle Dynamics (`effects-manager.ts`)
- **Stump Splintering Physics:** Off-stump / leg-stump carts wheel with angular velocity $\omega \in [0.1, 0.3]\text{ rad/tick}$, linear velocity $v_x, v_y$, and alpha fade decay.
- **Boundary Ripples:** Dual concentric expanding rings pulse outward from boundary contact point on FOURs and SIXes.
- **Celebration Particle Systems:** High-velocity multicolored confetti bursts on wickets and milestone boundaries.
- **Broadcast Toast Overlays:** Dynamic TV popups ("FOUR", "MAXIMUM 6", "WICKET - BOWLED", "FREE HIT") rendered directly with high-contrast typography.

### 3.5 Television Scoreboard HUD (`CricketVisualizerCanvas.tsx`)
- **Lower-Third Broadcast Overlay:**
  - Real-time score, target, current run rate (CRR), and required run rate (RRR).
  - Striker and non-striker scorecards (runs, balls faced, strike rate, boundaries).
  - Current bowler figures (overs, maidens, runs conceded, wickets taken, economy).
  - Wagon strip displaying the last 6 balls with color-coded outcomes (Red = Wicket, Gold = Boundary, Cyan = Dot).
- **Playback Controls:** Play, Pause, Step Next Ball, and 5 speed presets (0.5x, 1x, 2x, 5x, 10x).
