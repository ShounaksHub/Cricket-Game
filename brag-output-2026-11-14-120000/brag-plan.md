# Brag Plan: Cricket Dream XI

## What is this app?
Cricket Dream XI is an offline, deterministic cricket management game where the player builds a team, makes captaincy calls, and watches a full match resolve ball by ball.

## The angle
Treat the game like a premium broadcast trailer for a captain's notebook: every format has its own pressure, and every tactical choice has consequences.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
“Pick a team. Own the innings.” lands over the deep blue-and-sand identity, immediately framing the player as captain rather than spectator.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A broadcast-style stadium visualizer turns the match into something you can read at a glance.
- T10, T20, ODI, Test, and deterministic replay appear as distinct cricket problems, not generic game modes.
- A clear Attack / Balanced / Defensive decision card shows the captain actively changing the match plan.

## Outro / punchline
“The match is yours.” resolves the trailer with a direct invitation to play the next over.

## User flow worth showing
Choose a team → set the match plan → watch the ball-by-ball result and adjust at the next decision point.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: archival cricket broadcast meets modern captain’s cockpit
- Interpretation: confident pacing, warm editorial typography, restrained impact, and real product imagery rather than abstract startup visuals.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#12343B` / `#0B2329`
- Accent: `#E1B382` sand tan and `#9BD88A` decision green
- Text: `#F5EBDD` warm paper
- Display font: Georgia editorial serif
- Body font: Arial sans-serif
- Strongest visual element: the stadium visualizer and the dark-blue / sand editorial card system.

## Share copy (draft)
Cricket Dream XI turns every over into a captaincy call: build your XI, choose the plan, and watch the same seed play out the same way every time.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support with an upbeat, low-volume music bed.
- Music: `WDL - Bob's Beat (Audio).mp3`, selected from the project’s existing music library.
- Music treatment: start immediately at low volume, carry the trailer, and let the final title breathe.
- Music cue guidance: unavailable; composition timing prioritizes readable scene landings over beat sync.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; no aggressive pulsing.
- SFX posture: intentionally sparse so the cricket visuals stay credible.
- Audio-coupled moments: score reveal and final title landings can accept restrained impact accents if added later.
- Restraint rule: no generic SaaS whooshes or over-scored UI noise.
