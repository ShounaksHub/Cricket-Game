## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an elite broadcast-grade sports management experience at the intersection of high-fidelity television graphics, editorial cricket journalism, and tactical video game telemetry. It rejects juvenile cartoon aesthetics and over-saturated esports neon, situating the user in an illuminated pavilion atmosphere at dusk.

### Target Audience & Tone
Engineered for cricket purists, fantasy tacticians, and simulation gamers who value deep metrics and rapid strategic decision-making. The tone is authoritative, calculated, immersive, and razor-sharp.

### Design Movement: Editorial Sports High-Contrast Minimalism
- **Atmospheric Obsidian Backdrops:** Deep charcoal foundations (`#101410`) paired with matte green-tinted charcoal elevation decks (`#181F19`, `#202920`) eliminate glare while projecting pitch ambiance.
- **Precision Hairlines:** Strict 1px low-contrast structural dividers (`#303A31`) define data cells, player attribute matrices, and scoreboard docks without visual heaviness.
- **Dynamic Field Accents:** Crisp cricket pitch emerald green (`#4CAF50`) commands primary confirmation flows, punctuated by electric warm lime (`#B7E36B`) for momentum telemetry and heraldic gold (`#D4AF37`) for captaincy and milestones.

## Colors

The palette operates in strict dark mode, balancing low-strain sustained viewing with instantaneous contrast for match-critical data points.

### Palette Architecture & Semantic Application
- **Primary (`#4CAF50`):** Pitch turf green for primary interactive triggers, validated squad checkpoints, confirmational states, and active toggle buttons.
- **Secondary (`#B7E36B`):** High-energy warm lime for live scoring surges, strike-rate momentum flags, ball tracking vectors, and player run-rate deltas.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37`):** Regal gold dedicated exclusively to captaincy badges `(C)`, tournament trophies, century indicators, and Player of the Match honors.
- **Neutral (`#101410`):** Pitch-night charcoal foundation providing maximum dynamic range for text and data overlays.
- **Named Surfaces & Boundaries:**
  - `surface-card` (`#181F19`): Standard player roster cell and match container background.
  - `surface-elevated` (`#202920`): Active tabs, selected list items, and tactical button surfaces.
  - `surface-recessed` (`#0C0F0C`): Numeric slot beds, tabular wells, and input fields.
  - `border-hairline` (`#303A31`): Ubiquitous 1px structural framing.
- **Named Match States:**
  - `wicket-red` (`#C92A2A`): Wicket dismissals, bowling hazards, invalid squad constraints, and red-ball Test badges.
  - `test-pink` (`#E64980`): Day/night Test match badges and twilight play phases.
  - `telemetry-blue` (`#38BDF8`): Bowler profile pill and international cohort tagging.
  - `warning-amber` (`#F59E0B`): Required run rate spikes and wicketkeeper status alerts.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a tri-font structure:
1. **Manrope** provides authoritative geometric presence for tournament headlines and roster categories.
2. **Inter** ensures crystalline readability across long-form commentary, tactical descriptions, and player biographic sheets.
3. **JetBrains Mono** enforces fixed-width numerical rigor for live scoreboards, overs tracking, bowler figures, and strike rates.

### Tabular Formatting Rules
- All numerical telemetry (`scoreboard-xl`, `scoreboard-md`, strike rates, economy figures, and overs) must activate font feature settings `tnum` (tabular numbers) to eliminate layout horizontal jitter during rapid live score updates.
- Micro tags (`label-caps`) are styled strictly in uppercase with positive letter spacing (`0.06em`) for rapid identification across mobile screen widths.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a fluid-responsive grid system with fixed gutters, maximizing visual density while preserving structural breathing room.

### Layout Architectures
- **Standard Roster & Management:** 12-column responsive layout capped at a maximum width of `1280px` (`margin-desktop: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`).
- **Live Match Command Center:** Capped at `1440px` with a 3-column operational split:
  - Left Console (25% width): Batting/bowling scorecards and partnership tracker.
  - Field Canvas (50% width): Top-down 2D ground visualizer, field placements, and ball trajectories.
  - Tactical HUD (25% width): Ball-by-ball commentary stream, bowler stamina meters, and shot-selection controls.
- **Squad Builder Split:** Left panel (55%) contains searchable player pools; right panel (45%) houses the Playing XI slots, team radar charts, and balance warnings.

### Viewport Adaptation
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked layout. Scoreboard snaps as a persistent sticky top bar. Squad selection transitions to a swipeable drawer. Margin reduces to `1rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Two-column layout with tabbed access to ball-by-ball feeds.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** Unfolded high-density telemetry views with simultaneous interactive matrices.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **tonal surface layering** combined with **crisp low-contrast outlines** and restrained ambient glows. Heavy blurred drop shadows are deliberately avoided.

### Surface Elevation Levels
- **Ground Floor Canvas (`#101410`):** Base screen level.
- **Level 1 Containers (`#181F19`):** Primary player cards, match scorecard banners, and settings groups. Outlined with a 1px border in `#303A31`.
- **Level 2 Interactive Elements (`#202920`):** Active card segments, nested stat chips, hovered rows, and active navigation pills. Outlined with `#435144`.
- **Level 3 Modals & Floating Docks (`#263127`):** Match simulation pause menus, tactical replacement overlays, and sticky bottom control sheets. Bound with 1px `#435144` and an ambient perimeter tint: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.

### Luminous Accent Depth
Interactive CTAs and live states employ faint, color-tinted ambient underglows rather than black shadows:
- Primary Green Button: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(76, 175, 80, 0.2)`
- Tactical Attack Button: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(183, 227, 107, 0.25)`
- Captaincy Trophy Pin: `box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`

## Shapes

The design system enforces balanced geometric roundedness (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline) to maintain a crisp, tactical gaming structure that remains ergonomic on touch devices.

### Radius Conventions
- **Standard (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Buttons, search inputs, dropdowns, and tactical action triggers.
- **Large (`1rem` / `16px`):** Player roster cards, scorecard panels, and simulation stage containers.
- **Extra Large (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Elevated modal dialogues and bottom-sheet command centers.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Role badges (`BAT`, `BOWL`), format tags (`T20`, `ODI`), match status markers, and rating chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Game Action:** Fill `#4CAF50` with `#0A0E0B` bold typography. Height: 44px (48px on mobile). Corner radius: `rounded` (8px). Mild ambient green glow. Subtle `scale(1.02)` transform on hover.
- **Secondary Tactical Action:** Surface `#181F19` with a 1px `#303A31` outline and `#F4F5F0` text. Hover shifts background to `#202920` and border to `#4CAF50`.
- **Destructive/Bench Action:** Surface `#3D0D0D` with border `#C92A2A` and `#F4F5F0` text. Used for releasing players or forfeiting matches.

### Badges & Role Chips
- **Player Role Badges:** Height 22px, `rounded-full` pill structure with `label-caps` monospace text.
  - *Batter:* Text `#B7E36B`, background `#202920`, border `#303A31`.
  - *Bowler:* Text `#38BDF8`, background `#0F2430`, border `#1E3A4C`.
  - *All-Rounder:* Text `#D4AF37`, background `#282310`, border `#4A3E1B`.
  - *Wicketkeeper:* Text `#F59E0B`, background `#291E0A`, border `#4A3515`.
- **Format Badges:**
  - *T10:* Bright gold text (`#FBBF24`) on `#261F0A`.
  - *T20:* Emerald green text (`#10B981`) on `#0C2B1D`.
  - *ODI:* Sky blue text (`#3B82F6`) on `#0E2342`.
  - *Test Red Ball:* Cherry crimson text (`#C92A2A`) on `#2E0F0F`.
  - *Test Pink Ball:* Magenta-rose text (`#E64980`) on `#33101E`.

### Cards & Player Cells
- **Roster Card:** `#181F19` background, 1px `#303A31` border, `rounded-lg` (16px), 16px internal padding. Top bar displays national flag indicator, player role badge, and gold overall rating pill (`92 OVR`). Center showcases player surname in `title-md` (`#F4F5F0`). Lower deck embeds a 3-cell metric matrix (`BAT`, `BOWL`, `FIELD`) using tabular monospaced numbers.
- **Live Scoreboard Module:** Panoramic gradient banner (`#12161E` to `#181F19`), bordered by `#303A31` with `#D4AF37` hairline highlights. Prominently centers live runs/wickets in `scoreboard-xl`, with CRR and RRR metrics flagged in lime (`#B7E36B`) and amber (`#F59E0B`).

### Form Elements & Inputs
- **Search Inputs:** Recessed background `#0C0F0C`, 1px `#303A31` border, 8px radius. Active focus switches border to `#D4AF37` with an offset outline ring `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`. Placeholder text in muted slate (`#6B756C`).
- **Tactical Intent Control:** 4-segment segmented selector (`Defend`, `Rotate`, `Attack`, `Smash`). Inactive segments render on `#181F19`; the active selection illuminates in `#202920` with an accent perimeter matching the strategy's risk level.

### Lists & Tables
- **Playing XI Roster Strip:** 11-row draggable strip with alternating subtle backgrounds (`#141A14` to `#181F19`). Features slot position indexing (`01`–`11`), name, role badge, quick captain `(C)` and vice-captain `(VC)` toggle switches, and wicketkeeping indicators.
- **Squad Validation HUD:** Persistent bottom notification bar. Signals missing squad requirements in crimson `#3D0D0D` with error lists, or lights up in `#0D4A38` emerald with a checkmark when the XI is match-legal.
